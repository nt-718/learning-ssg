# 第10章 営業コミュニケーション

営業では「説明」よりも「質問」が重要です。

## 10.1 Discovery

> Could you walk me through your current process?

> What challenges are you currently facing?

> What are your main priorities this year?

> How are you handling this today?

## 10.2 深掘り

> How often does that happen?

> How much time does that currently take?

> Who is most affected by this issue?

> What happens if the problem isn't solved?

## 10.3 Value Proposition

機能：

> Our system uses AI.

価値：

> Our system automatically screens routine inquiries, which can reduce the workload on your recruiting team.

さらに：

> This allows recruiters to spend more time speaking directly with high-priority candidates.

## 10.4 Closing

> Would it make sense to run a pilot?

> Would you be interested in seeing a demo?

> Shall we discuss next steps?

## 演習

1. 現状プロセスを聞く。
2. 最大の課題を聞く。
3. 発生頻度を聞く。
4. コストを聞く。
5. 影響を聞く。
6. 理想状態を聞く。
7. AI機能を価値に変換して説明。
8. デモを提案。
9. PoCを提案。
10. 次回商談につなげる。

## ケーススタディ5　採用SaaS営業

Sales:

> Could you walk me through how your team currently handles candidate inquiries?

Customer:

> Recruiters answer most questions manually by email.

Sales:

> Roughly how much time does that take?

Customer:

> Probably ten to fifteen hours a week across the team.

Sales:

> And what types of questions come up most often?

Customer:

> Mostly questions about the selection process, benefits, working hours, and interview preparation.

Sales:

> That sounds like an area where automation could help. Our system can answer common candidate questions using company-approved information while escalating more complex questions to recruiters.

Customer:

> How much could it reduce our workload?

Sales:

> That depends on inquiry volume, but based on similar use cases, we would first measure the percentage of questions that can be handled automatically during a pilot.

## 学習ポイント

優れた営業英語は、

「当社にはAIがあります」

ではなく、

Customer problem  
↓
Business impact  
↓
Solution  
↓
Expected outcome

で構成します。
