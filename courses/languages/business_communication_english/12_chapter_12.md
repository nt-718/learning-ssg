# 第12章 Interview English

## 面接官

> Could you tell me a little about yourself?

> Could you walk me through your experience?

> What attracted you to this role?

> Can you tell me about a difficult problem you solved?

## 12.1 STAR

Situation

Task

Action

Result

例：

> In my previous role, our support team was receiving over 500 inquiries per week.

> I was responsible for improving response time.

> I analyzed the inquiry data and introduced a new categorization and routing process.

> As a result, average response time fell by 35%.

## 12.2 Behavioral Interview

> Tell me about a time when...

は非常によく使われます。

> Tell me about a time when you disagreed with your manager.

> Tell me about a time when you had to work under pressure.

## 12.3 数字を使う

弱い：

> I improved sales.

強い：

> I increased monthly sales by approximately 20% over six months.

## 演習

1. 1分自己紹介。
2. 志望理由。
3. 最大の強み。
4. 改善したい点。
5. 困難を乗り越えた経験。
6. チームで対立した経験。
7. リーダー経験。
8. 失敗経験。
9. 成果を数字付きで説明。
10. 面接官への質問を3つ作る。
