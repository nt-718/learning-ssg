# 第16章 総合ケーススタディ

## ケーススタディ6　海外チームとの開発プロジェクト

あなたは日本側のプロジェクトマネージャーです。

海外開発チームから次の連絡を受けました。

> We may need some more time for the API integration.

これは非常に曖昧です。

あなたは以下を確認する必要があります。

- 何が原因か
- どれくらい遅れるか
- リリースへの影響
- 対応案

## Reply

> Thanks for the update.

> Could you clarify what is causing the delay and how much additional time you expect to need?

> I'd also like to understand whether this affects the planned release date.

> If possible, please send us an updated timeline and proposed next steps by 3 p.m. today.

## ケーススタディ7　上司へのリスク報告

状況：

重要プロジェクトが遅延する可能性が30〜40%ある。

悪い報告：

> Everything is okay, but there might be a slight delay.

これではリスクが伝わりません。

改善：

> The project is currently on schedule, but there is a significant risk of delay.

> The external API integration is three days behind plan. If the vendor does not complete the fix by Wednesday, our release may be delayed by approximately one week.

> We are currently evaluating whether we can proceed with a temporary workaround.

## ケーススタディ8　部下の成果物が不十分

悪い：

> This isn't good enough. Please redo it.

改善：

> Thanks for putting this together.

> The overall structure works well, but I think the analysis needs more evidence to support the recommendation.

> In particular, could you add customer data and competitor examples to sections 2 and 3?

> If possible, please send me the revised version by tomorrow afternoon.

## ケーススタディ9　顧客から無理な要求

Customer:

> We need the new feature by Friday.

あなた：

> I understand that Friday is important for your launch.

> Based on the remaining development and testing work, however, we would not be able to deliver a production-ready version by Friday without increasing the risk of defects.

> We could offer two alternatives: a limited beta version on Friday, or the complete version next Wednesday.

> Which option would work better for you?

## ケーススタディ10　経営会議で提案する

> I recommend increasing our investment in customer retention.

> There are two main reasons.

> First, our acquisition cost has increased by 35% over the past year.

> Second, customers who remain with us for more than twelve months generate almost three times as much lifetime value as new customers.

> Therefore, I propose shifting 15% of our acquisition budget toward retention initiatives.
