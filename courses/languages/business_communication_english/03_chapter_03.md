# 第3章 英語における丁寧さ

## 3.1 日本語の敬語とは違う

英語には日本語の尊敬語・謙譲語のような体系はありません。

代わりに、

- modal verb
- indirectness
- softener
- appreciation
- explanation

などを使います。

## 3.2 依頼の強さ

### 最も直接的

> Send me the report.

命令。

### 少し柔らかい

> Please send me the report.

### 標準的

> Could you send me the report?

### より丁寧

> Could you please send me the report?

### 遠回し

> Would it be possible for you to send me the report?

## 3.3 Pleaseの誤解

日本人は、

> Please = 丁寧

と覚えがちです。

しかし、

> Please send this today.

は状況によってはかなり強い要求です。

対して、

> Could you send this by the end of today?

の方が柔らかく感じられる場合があります。

## 3.4 Softener

### slightly

> The schedule may be slightly too aggressive.

### a little

> This may be a little difficult.

### perhaps

> Perhaps we should consider another option.

### I think

> I think we may need more time.

### I'm not sure

> I'm not sure this approach will scale.

## 3.5 反対意見のレベル

強：

> I disagree.

標準：

> I don't think that's the best option.

柔：

> I'm not sure that's the best option.

さらに柔：

> I see your point, but I wonder if there might be another approach.

### 章末問題3

1. ［実務基礎］Send it.
2. ［実務基礎］You are wrong.
3. ［実務基礎］This plan is impossible.
4. ［実務基礎］Explain this.
5. ［実務基礎］Do it again.
6. ［実務基礎］I don't like this design.
7. ［実務基礎］You misunderstood.
8. ［実務基礎］You made a mistake.
9. ［実務基礎］This schedule is bad.
10. ［実務基礎］Change this immediately.

### 解答・解説3

1. Could you send it to me?
2. I see it a little differently.
3. I think the current plan may be difficult to execute within the proposed timeline.
4. Could you walk me through this?
5. Could you revise this section?
6. I'm not sure this design fully supports our objective.
7. I think there may have been a misunderstanding.
8. It looks like there may be an error here.
9. I'm concerned that the schedule may be too aggressive.
10. Could you prioritize this change?
