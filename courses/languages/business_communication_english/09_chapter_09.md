# 第9章 交渉英語

交渉では「要求」だけではなく、

- Interest
- Constraint
- Option
- Trade-off
- Agreement

を扱います。

## 9.1 PositionとInterest

Position：

> We need a 20% discount.

Interest：

> We need to keep the total annual cost below $100,000.

後者まで分かれば別の解決案を考えられます。

## 9.2 条件付き提案

> If you commit to a two-year contract, we can offer a 15% discount.

これは交渉で非常に重要な型です。

> If you..., we can...

## 9.3 譲歩

> We may be able to...

> We could consider...

> We're willing to...

## 9.4 拒否

> I'm afraid that wouldn't work for us.

> That would be difficult under the current budget.

> We wouldn't be able to agree to that condition.

## 9.5 Counteroffer

> Would you consider a 10% discount instead?

> What if we reduced the price by 10% and extended the contract to two years?

## 演習

1. 10%値引きを提案。
2. 2年契約なら15%値引き。
3. 相手の要求を丁寧に断る。
4. 代替案を出す。
5. 契約期間について質問。
6. 予算制約を説明。
7. 「その条件なら検討できます」。
8. 「価格以外の条件も検討しましょう」。
9. 「これが当社の最終提案です」。
10. 合意内容をまとめる。

## ケーススタディ4　SaaS価格交渉

Customer:

> Your proposal is above our budget. We'd need at least a 20% discount.

Vendor:

> I understand. Could I ask what budget range you're working with?

Customer:

> We need to keep the annual cost under $80,000.

Vendor:

> In that case, we may be able to adjust the package rather than simply reducing the price.

Customer:

> What would that look like?

Vendor:

> If we reduce the number of premium support hours and move to a two-year agreement, we can bring the annual cost down to $78,000.

Customer:

> That could work.

Vendor:

> Great. I'll send you a revised proposal today.
